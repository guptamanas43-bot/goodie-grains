About ROASTANA

A premium Indian snack brand built on the belief that healthy food should never compromise on flavour.

Who We Are

ROASTANA by Goodie Grains LLP is a premium snack brand founded in 2023 with a clear purpose—to redefine everyday snacking by bringing together taste, nutrition, and quality.

Rooted in India and designed for global markets, ROASTANA focuses on oven-baked snacks, clean ingredients, and innovative flavours that appeal to modern, health-conscious consumers.

Our products are developed to meet domestic and international quality expectations, making them reliable, export-ready, and scalable for long-term partnerships.

How ROASTANA Began

ROASTANA wasn't born in a boardroom or out of a trend. It began with frustration.

As a health-conscious foodie, the founder struggled to find snacks that were both flavourful and genuinely healthy. Most options in the market leaned toward either bland "health foods" or indulgent snacks lacking nutritional value.

That gap became the foundation of ROASTANA—a brand created to offer snacks that are delicious, nutritious, and flavourful, without shortcuts or compromises.
